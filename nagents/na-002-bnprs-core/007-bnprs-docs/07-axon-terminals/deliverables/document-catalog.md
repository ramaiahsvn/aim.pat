# Business Document Catalog — Software Company (India)

> Master taxonomy of document categories an India-based software company needs.
> Prepared by **bnprs-docs** (na-002/007). Catalog only — templates to follow.
> Jurisdiction: **India** (DPDP Act 2023, POSH Act 2013, Companies Act 2013, GST, PF/ESI/Gratuity).

**Priority legend** — 🔴 Core (essential/statutory) · 🟡 Recommended (standard practice) · ⚪ As-needed (situational)

---

## 1. HR — Employment Lifecycle

| Document | Purpose | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Offer Letter | Extends a role, comp & joining date; pre-appointment | 🔴 |
| Appointment Letter / Employment Agreement | Binding terms of employment on joining | 🔴 |
| Employee NDA & Confidentiality Agreement | Protects company confidential information | 🔴 |
| IP Assignment Agreement | Assigns code/inventions created by employee to company | 🔴 |
| Non-Compete / Non-Solicitation | Limits post-exit competition & poaching | 🟡 |
| Internship Offer & Agreement | Terms for interns / trainees | 🟡 |
| Probation Confirmation Letter | Confirms employment after probation | 🔴 |
| Promotion / Increment Letter | Records role or salary revision | 🟡 |
| Transfer Letter | Location / department / entity transfer | ⚪ |
| Warning Letter / Show-Cause Notice | Disciplinary record | 🟡 |
| Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) | Structured performance remediation | 🟡 |
| Termination Letter | Ends employment with cause/notice | 🔴 |
| Resignation Acceptance Letter | Acknowledges an employee's resignation | 🟡 |
| Relieving Letter | Confirms exit & last working day | 🔴 |
| Experience / Service Certificate | Certifies tenure & role for the employee | 🔴 |
| Salary Slip / Payslip | Monthly earnings & deductions breakup | 🔴 |
| Full & Final Settlement Statement | Final dues on exit | 🔴 |
| Bonafide / Employment Verification Letter | Confirms employment for banks/visas | 🟡 |

## 3. Legal & Commercial — Client / Vendor

| Document | Purpose | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Master Services Agreement (MSA) | Umbrella terms for all client work | 🔴 |
| Statement of Work (SOW) | Scope, deliverables, timeline, price per engagement | 🔴 |
| Service Level Agreement (SLA) | Uptime, response/resolution commitments | 🔴 |
| Software License Agreement (EULA) | End-user rights to licensed software | 🔴 |
| SaaS Subscription Agreement | Terms for a hosted/subscription product | 🔴 |
| Terms of Service (ToS) | Public terms for product/website users | 🔴 |
| Privacy Policy | **DPDP Act 2023** — data collection & use disclosure | 🔴 |
| Data Processing Agreement (DPA) | Processor/controller data obligations | 🔴 |
| Mutual NDA | Two-way confidentiality with clients/vendors | 🔴 |
| Vendor / Supplier Agreement | Terms for buying goods/services | 🟡 |
| Consulting / Contractor Agreement | Independent-contractor engagement | 🔴 |
| Reseller / Channel Partner Agreement | Partner sales/distribution terms | ⚪ |
| Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) | Non-binding intent between parties | 🟡 |
| Letter of Intent (LoI) | Pre-contract commitment | 🟡 |
