1. Time elapsed after the final APDU transmission

To make sure we measure what you need, here is how our session is structured. Every APDU is relayed by our kiosk agent to the card through IC_Input, and our latency figures are taken strictly around the IC_Input call — nothing else is inside the measurement.

In the success path our host adds no deliberate delay between APDUs; the only wait in the code is a 300 ms settle before a retry after a 6985. After the last APDU of a personalization we call Card_EjectEx (good card) or Card_Control(0x36) (reject bin) immediately.

We have now instrumented the agent to report, on every run and to the millisecond:

• absolute timestamp of every APDU (HH:MM:SS.mmm)
• lat= — duration of the IC_Input call itself
• gap= — host-side idle between one APDU returning and the next being sent
• time from the final APDU to Card_EjectEx, and the duration of the eject call

The exact figures will come with the logs in the next runs. One point worth flagging in advance: our APDU stream is generated by a remote server, so a gap= value of roughly 50–200 ms is normal network round-trip and is outside the lat= measurement — the 3.5–4 s we reported for DGI A004/A002 is time spent inside IC_Input alone.

If by "after the final APDU transmission" you meant something else — for example how long the card remains powered at the contact station before ejection — please say so and we will report that figure specifically.
